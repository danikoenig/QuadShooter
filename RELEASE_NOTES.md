Free 4-slot one-shot layering sampler for macOS (Apple Silicon), VST3 and Audio Unit. By Dani Koenig.

**Download `QuadShooter-Installer.pkg`** and double-click it. macOS blocks it the first time: open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Full guide: see INSTALL.txt in the ZIP or in the repository.

### New in v0.4.1
- **Uninstaller**: `QuadShooter-Uninstaller.pkg` removes Quad Shooter with a double-click (VST3, Audio Unit, Standalone app). Your projects and library stay untouched.

### Features
- 4 sample slots A, B, C, D with pitch detection and a fine pitch slider (1-cent steps)
- XY Mix to blend the four slots
- Key Sync: pitch all slots to the lead slot (Nearest / Exact), root note by menu or typed in
- Edit row per slot: Start/End, Level, Pan, Hold/Decay, Pitch, Tone, Low/High Cut, VelSens
- Chromatic mode (C3 = original pitch)
- Amp: Attack, Decay, Release
- FX chain in any order: Delay (Mono / Ping-Pong, tempo synced or free), Reverb (room to big), Clipper with Auto Gain
- Output Gain into a Limiter (-1 dB) with GR meter
- Library: drop a folder, click to listen, drag a sample onto a slot

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or newer.
